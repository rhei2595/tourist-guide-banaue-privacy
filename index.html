Privacy Policy

Tourist Guide Banaue

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tourist Guide Banaue handles information when you use the app. The app provides tourism information about attractions, restaurants, accommodations, transportation, travel tips, maps, and Favorites.

2. Information We Handle

Account information: If you register or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and authentication credentials. The app may also store the display name associated with your account. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not intended to be stored as plain text by the app.

Favorites: Favorite tourism items can be stored on your device so they remain available locally. When you are signed in and connectivity is available, the app may synchronize Favorites with Firebase Cloud Firestore under your account.

App data stored on your device: Tourism listings and related app information are stored locally to support offline use. The app may also keep preferences and pending synchronization information on your device.

Location: The Android app manifest declares location permissions. The map screen currently uses a fixed Banaue map center; this policy does not claim that precise GPS location is collected. The app's permission requirements should be reviewed before release.

3. Services and Third Parties

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore: These Google Firebase services support account sign-in and cloud synchronization of Favorites. Information handled by these services is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation.

OpenStreetMap map tiles: When the map loads online tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org, your device makes requests to that service. The tile provider may receive technical request information such as your IP address and the requested map tiles. Map tiles may not load without an internet connection.

External links: The app may link to third-party websites or social media pages for tourism businesses. Those services have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide account access, keep account details associated with your profile, save and synchronize Favorites, and support the app's features. We do not state that the app sells personal information.

5. Offline Use and Storage

Some tourism information and Favorites may remain available from local storage without internet access. Account authentication and cloud synchronization require connectivity. Changes made offline may remain on the device until the app can synchronize them.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal information by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page. Requests will be reviewed and processed by the app operator. Some information may remain temporarily in backups or service logs where applicable, or where retention is required by law.

Deleting the app from your device may not delete information already associated with your Firebase account. Please use the account-deletion process to request account deletion.

7. Children's Privacy

Tourist Guide Banaue is a general tourism information app and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Security

We rely on the security features of the services used by the app and take reasonable steps within our control to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
